Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Watermelon Maker: Fruit Puzzle (the "App") provided by WookStudio (the "Service Provider").

1. Information We Collect

The App can be used without signing up. The Service Provider does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number, and does not store it on its own servers.

Game progress information (best score, Fruit Gem balance, game settings, game in progress, etc.) is stored on your device so that your game can be saved.

Google Play Games Services (Ranking)
The App uses Google Play Games Services for the global ranking feature. When you sign in to Google Play Games, your player ID, game nickname, profile image, and game score are processed through Google, and your game score is stored on the Google Play Games Services leaderboard. On the leaderboard, your nickname, profile image, and score may be shown to other users as part of the ranking. The Service Provider does not store this information on any separate server of its own.

In addition, through third-party services used for serving ads and processing in-app purchases (Google AdMob, Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, etc.), advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, app activity, and purchase information may be processed automatically.

This information may be used to provide rankings, serve ads, process in-app purchases, restore purchases, improve service quality, and analyze errors. The specific handling is governed by the privacy policy of each third-party service provider.

* The App does not collect your precise location.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Saving game progress data
  • Providing game features
  • Keeping your settings
  • Providing the global ranking
  • Serving and optimizing ads
  • Processing in-app purchases and restoring purchases
  • Improving service quality and analyzing errors
  • Preventing misuse

3. Data Storage and Processing

Game data such as your best score, Fruit Gem balance, and settings is stored in your device's internal storage and is not sent to or stored on the Service Provider's own servers.

However, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your game score is sent to and stored on Google Play Games Services (Google's servers) for the ranking feature.

Depending on your device's backup settings, app data may be included in and kept in your Google account backup, and may be restored when you reinstall the App. You can change backup settings in your device settings.

If you uninstall the App or clear its stored data, the game progress data stored on your device may be deleted as well, and deleted data may not be recoverable.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to serve banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads (ads you watch to receive Fruit Gems or to continue a game). These ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

In the course of serving ads, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and ad interaction information may be processed.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or limit ad personalization, in the ads privacy settings on your Android device.

6. In-App Purchases

The App includes in-app purchases for removing ads and for buying in-game currency (Fruit Gems).

All payments are processed through the Google Play billing system, and the Service Provider does not directly collect or store payment information such as credit card numbers or bank account numbers.

To process and restore purchases, the purchased item, purchase status, order identifier, or purchase token may be processed.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress information is kept on your device.

You can delete the game data stored on your device by clearing the App's stored data or uninstalling the App.

Scores and game activity information stored in Google Play Games Services are kept separately under Google's policies, and you can request deletion of your game activity information in the settings of the Google Play Games app.

Google Play purchase history and information processed by third-party services may be kept separately under each service provider's policies.

8. Your Rights

You can exercise your rights regarding the handling of your information in the following ways.

  • Clear the App's stored data
  • Uninstall the App
  • Sign out of Google Play Games and request deletion of game activity information
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier
  • Change ad personalization settings
  • Check your Google Play purchase history and payment settings
  • Contact us by email about privacy matters

9. Children's Privacy
